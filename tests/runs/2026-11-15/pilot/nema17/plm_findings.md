# PLM findings — NEMA-17 stepper motor integration, extruder drive (stage 5)

Reviewer: Goldfish PLM review. Sources: `nema17_spec.md`,
`nema17_facts.md`, `timing_pulley_facts.md`, and the cited STEP files
`~/workspace/cad-viewer/test-fixtures/nema17.step` and
`~/workspace/cad-viewer/test-fixtures/timing_pulley.step`.
Both STEP files were re-measured independently with
`/tmp/measure.py` (build123d / OCCT 8.0); every number in the facts
files reproduced exactly (solids, bounding boxes, volumes, face
counts, cylindrical-face diameters).

## Findings

### Blocker

- **Blocker — dataset `NEMA17_MOTOR` Rev B (§6).** The spec cites
  the motor source file as dataset `NEMA17_MOTOR` Rev B, but the
  file's product version is "NEMA-17 Stepper Motor v11" — the cited
  revision does not match the file, so the spec cannot be traced to
  the measured model.
  Fix: correct the cited revision to the file's version (v11), or
  cite the dataset revision that actually matches the measured file.

### Major

- **Major — CAD-system / dataset provenance (§6).** The spec claims
  the motor is a Siemens NX dataset, but `nema17.step` is authored
  by Adafruit (FILE_NAME path is an Adafruit Dropbox folder,
  generated by STEP Tools ST-Developer, 2018-07-07) and contains
  zero references to NX or Siemens.
  Fix: record the motor as a vendor-supplied Adafruit STEP import,
  not an NX-authored dataset, and name the import record.

- **Major — dataset `NEMA17_EXTRUDER` Rev C (§6).** The top-level
  assembly dataset is cited with a revision but no source file, no
  product name, no part count and no envelope, so Rev C cannot be
  verified against any file.
  Fix: cite the assembly file with its measured contents, or remove
  the revision claim.

- **Major — timing pulley dataset (§2, §6).** The mating part is
  cited only by filename (`timing_pulley.step`); it has no dataset
  name, no revision and no change-control status, although the file's
  product is "Aluminum Timing Pulley v1".
  Fix: assign the pulley a dataset name and revision and state its
  change-control status.

- **Major — mounting plate dataset (§6 vs §8).** The mounting plate
  dataset is stated "released" but is never named, has no revision
  and no cited file, and §8 assumption (2) says its drawing is still
  TO BE OBTAINED — a released dataset whose drawing is not on file
  is a contradiction.
  Fix: name the dataset and revision and put its drawing on file, or
  change its status to unreleased.

- **Major — motor dataset change status (§6).** The motor dataset is
  "under change request CR-207" with no CR status stated
  (open / approved / implemented) and no statement of which revision
  CR-207 applies to, while the spec depends on the cited revision
  for acceptance.
  Fix: state CR-207's status and whether the cited revision is
  pre- or post-change.

- **Major — pulley retention interface (§2, §4, §9 vs
  `timing_pulley.step`).** The spec states retention is a press fit
  onto the D-flat shaft, but the pulley bore is a plain Ø5.00 mm
  round bore with no flat, no set-screw hole and no keyway (measured:
  a single Ø5.00 cylinder, no other bore feature), and shaft and bore
  are both stated Ø5.00 with no interference value — the D-flat
  provides no drive and the press fit has no specified interference.
  Fix: specify the bore/shaft interference and an anti-rotation
  feature (set screw or broached flat), or change the retention claim.

- **Major — 10,000-hour service life (§1 vs §8).** The requirement
  "run for 10,000 hours without service" has no verification method
  in §8 — no life test, accelerated test, or supplier data is named.
  Fix: add a verification method to §8 (supplier life-test data or
  an accelerated test).

- **Major — recycled-content requirement (§5).** The target is ≥ 20%
  recycled aluminium in the end caps, but the same section states the
  end caps are virgin aluminium with no recycled-content data
  supplied — the requirement is contradicted by the stated material
  and has no verification method.
  Fix: obtain recycled-content data from the supplier, or drop or
  reword the target.

- **Major — electrical ratings (§2 vs §8, §9).** 1.5 A/phase and
  12 V nominal are stated with no verification method in §8, and §9
  admits current is a requirement, not a verified value, with no
  supplier datasheet on file.
  Fix: add a verification method (supplier datasheet on file) or mark
  the ratings as assumptions with named closing evidence.

### Minor

- **Minor — RoHS compliance (§5).** RoHS compliance is claimed by
  the supplier with the certificate not on file and no verification
  method in §8.
  Fix: file the RoHS certificate or add a verification step to §8.

- **Minor — cost basis (§7).** The $12.80 purchase price cites no
  supplier quotation; with no datasheet on file (§9) the price is
  UNVERIFIED.
  Fix: cite the supplier quotation or mark the cost UNVERIFIED.

- **Minor — "Motor part count: 5 solids" (§6).** The file's 5 solids
  are 4 overlapping body construction sections plus the shaft, not
  5 separable parts, so calling them the part count misstates the
  assembly.
  Fix: describe the count as 5 solids in the imported model, not a
  part count.

- **Minor — material composition (§3).** Steel laminations,
  aluminium end caps, copper windings and NdFeB magnets are stated
  with no source; the composition is not verifiable from the STEP
  file and no supplier datasheet is on file (§9).
  Fix: cite the supplier datasheet or material declaration, or mark
  the composition UNVERIFIED.

## Notes — UNVERIFIED, not findings

- Holding torque ≥ 40 N·cm, step angle 1.8° ± 5%, shaft runout
  ≤ 0.05 mm and case temperature ≤ 80 °C (§1) each have a named
  verification method in §8 but no evidence on file; they are open
  requirements, not verified values.
- The spec contains no internal "see §" cross-references and no
  numbered figures, so there were no section or figure references
  to check.

## Controls passed

- Motor body 42.30 × 35.00 × 42.30 mm (§2, §4) — measured body
  Y −16.50..18.50, envelope 42.30 × 55.00 × 42.30 mm.
- Overall length with shaft 55.00 mm (§2) — measured Y −16.50..38.50.
- Shaft Ø5.00 mm × 24.0 mm long, 20.0 mm protrusion (§2, §4) —
  measured Y 14.50..38.50, front face at Y 18.50.
- D-flat 0.5 mm deep × 17.75 mm long, starting 2.0 mm from the front
  face (§2, §4) — measured plane Z = 2.00 vs shaft surface Z = 2.50,
  Y 20.50..38.25.
- Mounting holes 4× Ø3.07 mm on 31.0 mm pitch (§2, §4) — measured
  centres (±15.50, ±15.50).
- Pilot recess Ø22.0 mm × 2.2 mm deep (§2, §4) — measured.
- Front cap shaft opening Ø10.0 mm (§2) — measured D10.00.
- Motor source file format STEP AP214 (§6) — measured header
  `AUTOMOTIVE_DESIGN { 1 0 10303 214 3 1 1 }`.
- Motor solid count 5 (§6) — measured 5 solids (see minor finding on
  calling this a part count).
- Pulley 36 tooth, bore Ø5.00 mm (§2) — measured 36 root segments
  Ø20.00 mm and 36 tip segments Ø22.40 mm, bore Ø5.00 mm.
- Cost arithmetic $12.80 + $1.70 = $14.50 (§7) — agrees.
- Verification methods named for holding torque, step accuracy,
  runout and thermal (§8) — present.
- Both open assumptions name their closing evidence (§8) — supplier
  datasheet torque curve, mounting plate drawing, both TO BE OBTAINED.
